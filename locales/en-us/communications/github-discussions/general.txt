Say hello and tell us how you use Agile ITIL Metrics

Welcome to the Agile ITIL Metrics Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the book.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Agile ITIL Metrics because I wanted a practical handbook for measuring the work of teams that build and run IT services the agile way, with the gaming vector and guardrail for every metric to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which one metric does your team track today, and how could it be gamed?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.
